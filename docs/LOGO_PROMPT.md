Direction: keep concept A (open book + speech bubble + Arabic letter ع). Palette: deep teal #105a67, coral #ff7a5c, white.

```
Design a clean, modern vector logo lockup for "Ilmak" (عِلمك), an Arabic-first online teaching marketplace.

Symbol: a single speech bubble shape whose inside forms an open book, with a bold, clearly readable Arabic letter ع (ain, with a small kasra mark below) in coral at its center. Colors: deep teal #105a67 and coral #ff7a5c on pure white. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D.

Wordmarks, side by side with the symbol:
1. Arabic: عِلمك in a modern, friendly Arabic sans-serif (Tajawal / Cairo style), teal, with the kasra visible.
2. Latin: "ilmak" all lowercase with a coral dot on the i, rounded geometric sans-serif, teal. It must NOT look like "llmak".

Also produce: (a) horizontal lockup with Arabic wordmark on the right and Latin below it, (b) a simplified app icon: rounded square, teal background, white speech bubble with coral ع only (no book detail), must stay readable at 32px, (c) a one-color white version on teal.
Layout: generous padding, centered on a white canvas. No taglines, no extra text.
```
